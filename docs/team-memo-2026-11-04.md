# Team memo
From Sam, written with Claude — November 4, 2026
Where an older note disagrees, this memo wins.

## Who does what
- Sam: owner. Final say on everything.
- Claude: overseer. Checks plans and finished work, asks questions, catches mistakes. Sam passes Claude's notes to the builders. Treat them as coming from Sam.
- School Site: builds the website (kirkSam/online-school, dotheimpossible.ai).
- App Builder: builds the app (kirkSam/vkole-app).

## The goal right now
Get the app ready for release in the app stores, as close to perfect as we can. Release was slowed down. It was never meant to stop. The Oct 26 note saying store work is "paused indefinitely" is wrong and is replaced by this memo. While getting ready we will find things to fix. That is expected. Report them and fix them as part of the work.

## How the app and the website relate
1. Engines are identical. Every VKOLE engine works the same way on the app and on the website: same questions, same leveling, same feedback, same wording. Engines will improve over time, but always on both sides together.
2. Student information is separate. The app and the website do not share student information: no levels, progress, answers, seen questions, session reports, student names, ages, or grades.
3. Only accounts are shared. The one thing shared is creating a parent account and signing in. One account works on both.

This is for legal and marketing reasons. It replaces older notes that say "all progress is logged on both" or "the website is the only account book."

Do not put the "private and not shared" promise back on any page until the separation is finished and Claude has verified it.

## Rules
1. An engine change is finished only when it works identically on both sides. New engines start in the app. Changes to existing engines start on the website, then the app.
2. Never add a new way for student information to move between app and website.
3. Nothing is sent to Apple or Google without Sam's OK.
4. If you find a problem, report it. Do not quietly work around it.
5. Plain words. Parent-facing text at about a 6th-grade reading level.
6. Every report to Sam uses the form in the Nov 4 message: what a parent will notice, what changed, also needed on the other side, safe to merge, and how it was checked.
